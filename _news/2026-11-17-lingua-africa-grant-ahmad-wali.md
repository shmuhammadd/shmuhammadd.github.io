🎉 My student, Ahmad Mustapha Wali, has received a **[LINGUA Africa](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/academic-program/lingua-africa-open-call-for-inclusive-ai-language-projects/)** research grant for the project *DialectBridge*, which will improve speech recognition across the dialects of Hausa. LINGUA Africa is led by the Masakhane African Languages Hub, the Gates Foundation, Microsoft AI for Good Lab, and Google. It funds open language resources, tools, and applications for African languages. Congratulations, Ahmad!
