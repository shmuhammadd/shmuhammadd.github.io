🏆 Our paper, [“How Well Does It Generalize? A Robustness Study of Hausa Machine-Generated Text Detection”](https://aclanthology.org/2026.winlp-main.5.pdf), won the **Best Paper Award** at the Widening NLP (WiNLP) Workshop at **EMNLP 2026** in Budapest. Congratulations to my co-authors Abdulhamid Abubakar, Faisal Muhammad Adam, Abubakar Auwal Khalid and Babangida Sani.
