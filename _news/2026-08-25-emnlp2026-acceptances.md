🎉 Happy to share that 6 papers have been accepted at **EMNLP 2026** (Budapest)! 5 as Main Conference papers and 1 to Findings of the ACL:

- **NaijaS2ST: A Multi-Accent Benchmark for Speech-to-Speech Translation in Low-Resource Nigerian Languages** (Main)
- **AfriScience-MT: Towards Decolonizing Science in Africa through Text Translation** (Main)
- **AFRILANGTUTOR: Advancing Language Tutoring and Culture Education in Low-Resource Languages with Large Language Models** (Findings)
- **INCLUDE-2.0: Disentangling Knowledge Gaps in Multilingual LLM Evaluation** (Main)
- **PINGPONG: A Natural Benchmark for Multi-Turn Code-Switching Dialogues** (Main)
- **AfriSUD: A Dependency Treebank Collection for Evaluating Models on African Languages** (Main)

Thanks to all my collaborators — see you in Budapest!
