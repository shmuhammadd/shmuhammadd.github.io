🇺🇸 I was awarded the **U.S.-Africa Frontiers Fellowship** by the National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine. The fellowship grew from my talk at the U.S.-Africa Frontiers symposium in Senegal, where I met Prof. Gloria Washington of Howard University. Her work on African American English is close to my research on low-resource African languages. In August, I visited **Howard University** as a Fellow and gave a talk to HCAI @ Howard and the Affective Biometrics Lab. Thanks to Gloria Washington, Anietie Andy, Girmaw Abebe Tadesse and Daniel Placht.
