🎉 My PhD student, Sukairaj Hafiz Imam, has received a **[Google PhD Fellowship](https://research.google/programs-and-events/phd-fellowship/recipients/)** for his work on automatic speech recognition (ASR) for African languages. He has also won a **[LINGUA Africa](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/academic-program/lingua-africa-open-call-for-inclusive-ai-language-projects/)** research grant for his project, *DialectBridge*, which will improve speech recognition across the dialects of Hausa. LINGUA Africa is led by the Masakhane African Languages Hub, the Gates Foundation, Microsoft AI for Good Lab, and Google, and funds open language resources, tools, and applications for African languages. Congratulations, Sukairaj!
